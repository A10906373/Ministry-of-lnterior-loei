# Jetpack on plugin-enabled plans

Our Jetpack plugin is automatically installed on all WordPress.com sites to give you essential features that secure, speed up, and grow your site. See our [Jetpack on WordPress.com](https://wordpress.com/support/jetpack/) guide for additional features available on all plans.

## About Jetpack

One benefit of hosting a website on WordPress.com is that your site includes all the essential features you need to run it. So, you don’t have to deal with the hassle of picking and choosing plugins and extra services to keep your site running smoothly and securely.

[Our Jetpack plugin](https://wordpress.com/plugins/jetpack) is pre-installed so you can concentrate on your site’s content while we take care of security and delivery to all your site visitors with the best performance in the world.

## Daily backups and restores

Jetpack will take a [full backup](https://wordpress.com/support/restore/) of your site every day. In addition, Jetpack also does incremental backups whenever something larger changes on your site, like installing a new plugin, for example.

On [eligible plans](https://wordpress.com/plans/), use any backup to restore your site to a previous point, ensuring your site’s content is always safe no matter what.

[Site backups and restores](https://wordpress.com/support/restore/)

## Plugin auto-updates

Forget about time-consuming plugin updates and let Jetpack update your plugins automatically when updates become available.

[Plugin updates](https://wordpress.com/support/plugins/update-a-plugin-or-theme//)

## Security scanning

Security on plugin-enabled websites hosted with WordPress.com is handled by [Jetpack Scan](https://wordpress.com/support/jetpack-scan/). You can read more about how we protect your site in [our dedicated security guide](https://wordpress.com/support/security/).

Jetpack Scan reads the content of plugins and theme files to find vulnerabilities and security threats like malware. The Jetpack Scan service also scans files uploaded by the site’s users to ensure everything is safe.

To learn more about security scanning in Jetpack Scan on WordPress.com, please review our dedicated Jetpack Scan support guide linked below.

[Security scanning](https://wordpress.com/support/jetpack-scan/)

## Can I deactivate Jetpack?

Sites hosted on WordPress.com **cannot deactivate Jetpack** (or [Akismet](https://wordpress.com/support/protect-from-spam/)), as doing so would break access to your site and remove the essential features they provide. Jetpack is automatically managed to ensure your site’s ultimate security and performance.

If you are experiencing an issue with another plugin on your site, the plugin’s developers may advise you to disable all plugins, including Jetpack. Jetpack is unlikely to cause issues; therefore, you won’t need to deactivate it.

However, you can deactivate specific Jetpack features you believe may be causing a conflict.

This section of the guide applies to sites with the WordPress.com [Business and Commerce plan](https://wordpress.com/plans/). If you have a Business plan, make sure to [activate it](https://wordpress.com/support/activate-your-business-plan/). For free sites and sites on the Personal and Premium plans, [upgrade your plan](https://wordpress.com/support/manage-purchases/upgrade-your-plan/) to access this feature.

To deactivate specific features of Jetpack, take the following steps:

1.  Visit your site’s [dashboard](https://wordpress.com/home/).
2.  Navigate to *Settings → General* and ensure the [admin interface](https://wordpress.com/support/navigate-wordpress-com/set-your-admin-interface-style/) is set to **“Classic style”**.
3.  Navigate to *Jetpack → My Jetpack*.
4.  Click the **Products** tab to view a list of all Jetpack features with toggles to enable or disable each module.
5.  Click the toggle switch next to any module in the list to activate or deactivate it.
