# Jetpack on WordPress.com

Jetpack is the ultimate WordPress plugin included in all websites on WordPress.com. This guide lists the Jetpack Essentials features included with Free, Personal, and Premium plan sites. See our [Jetpack on Plugin-Enabled Sites](https://wordpress.com/support/jetpack/jetpack-on-plugin-enabled-sites/) guide for additional features available on higher plans.

## Jetpack Social

Connect your WordPress.com site to various social networking services so that you can automatically share (and re-share) your posts on many social media platforms.

[Jetpack Social](https://wordpress.com/support/post-automatically-to-social-media/)

## Comments

The Jetpack commenting features replace the default WordPress comment form with an improved system that includes social media login options, comment likes, and more.

[Comments](https://wordpress.com/support/comments/)

## Share buttons

Jetpack includes sharing buttons for all major social networks so readers can easily spread the word about your content. On a block theme, add them with the [Sharing Buttons block](https://wordpress.com/support/wordpress-editor/blocks/sharing-buttons-block/). On a classic theme, see [Add social share buttons (classic themes)](https://wordpress.com/support/sharing/).

![Sharing buttons.](https://en-support.files.wordpress.com/2023/08/sharing-buttons-more.png)

## Site activity

The activity log is a complete record of everything on your site, giving you a clear, transparent way to monitor changes. Free plans are limited to viewing the past 20 events, whereas [WordPress.com paid plans](https://wordpress.com/plans/) have access to their complete site activity.

[Site Activity](https://wordpress.com/support/activity/)

## Custom content types

All WordPress sites include [pages](https://wordpress.com/support/pages/) and [posts](https://wordpress.com/support/posts/) as the main forms of content. The Jetpack plugin includes two additional content types on all sites hosted with WordPress.com: [portfolios](https://wordpress.com/support/portfolios/) and [testimonials](https://wordpress.com/support/testimonials/). You can use these custom content types to showcase your work and share client feedback without installing extra plugins.

## Optimized performance

Jetpack provides several performance features to ensure your pages load fast and your visitors have a smooth browsing experience. Features like the [Content Delivery Network (CDN)](https://wordpress.com/support/site-accelerator-cdn/) and lazy-load images are included in all plans, with the ability to turn these features on or off included in higher-level plans.

[Performance Settings](https://wordpress.com/support/performance-settings)

## Jetpack Stats

Jetpack Stats includes graphs, charts, and data to show you how many visits your site gets, which posts and pages are the most popular, where your traffic is coming from, and much more. Understand the most important information about your site through a clear and attractive interface.

[Jetpack Stats](https://wordpress.com/support/stats/)

## Akismet anti-spam

Spam mitigation is powered by our Akismet service, so your blog posts, forms, and comments on your site are less likely to be overrun with spam. You can see how much spam Akismet has blocked for you under *Jetpack → Akismet Anti-Spam.*

[Spam Mitigation](https://wordpress.com/support/protect-from-spam/)
